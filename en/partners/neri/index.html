Official partner · Italy

Neri. Italian since 1962.

Lampposts, lanterns and street furniture with its own foundry, design team and Light Room. Strapitec is an official partner and supplies the full collection.

1962

The beginning

Neri is founded in Longiano, in Italy's Emilia-Romagna region, as a foundry for lampposts.

Foundry

In-house production

From cast iron to aluminium: drawing, mould, casting and finishing under one roof.

Lab

Tested to the extreme

In-house labs test for weathering, mechanical load and electrical safety.

SOM

World-class architecture

With Skidmore, Owings & Merrill, Neri developed the Nebula family: lighting and furniture from a single idea.

Strapitec

Official partner

For more than eight years Strapitec has supplied and advised on the Neri collection in the Netherlands and Belgium.

The foundry

Where every light begins

Neri has grown into a leading manufacturer of cast-iron lampposts and luminaires. Its own design team, foundry and test laboratories keep quality at one level, from classic lantern to contemporary family.
