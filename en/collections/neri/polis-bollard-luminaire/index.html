Polis Bollard

Polis Bollard is a lighting bollard by Neri, built up from two conical volumes that together form a recognisable silhouette. It is available in the Pure and Carve versions and in three heights: 400, 800 and 1000 millimetres. It marks and lights walking routes, entrances and outdoor spaces around buildings. Photometric files and technical documentation are available to support the lighting design.

Pure and Carve: two characters

Every height of the Polis Bollard is available in two versions, Pure and Carve, each giving its own character to the same silhouette. This lets you select the appearance that suits the architecture of each project while keeping the recognisable form.
