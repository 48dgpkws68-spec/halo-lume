Chara litter bin

The Chara litter bin takes the form of a tapered cylinder in hot-dip galvanised steel, carried on a matching tapered steel post with a decorative end element. An inner container in polyethylene makes emptying straightforward. The bin is available with a cover and, if desired, with an ashtray, forming a coherent furniture line together with the Chara bench and bollard.
