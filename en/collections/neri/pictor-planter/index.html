Pictor planter

The Pictor planter is part of the Pictor street furniture range, designed together with the architecture firm Lombardini22. The square planter in painted stainless steel is available in three heights: 450, 600 and 900 millimetres. Four self-levelling feet compensate for uneven paving, keeping the planter stable on irregular surfaces. Placed individually or in groups, it brings greenery and structure to streets and squares.
