Propus pole

The Propus pole is a tapered lighting column with a round section, built from hot-dip galvanised steel tubes. At the base sit a decorative cast iron element and a hatch giving access to the terminal block. The pole carries up to two light sources and forms the basis of the Propus system, which you compose yourself with a top bracket and luminaire of choice. The design by Alfredo Farnè makes the pole a fit for streets, squares and residential areas that call for a well-kept streetscape.
