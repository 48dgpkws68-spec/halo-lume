Matar top

This top completes the Matar system and carries the luminaire on a lateral arm. It is made of hot-dip galvanised steel and follows the design language of Makio Hasuike. A single version accommodates up to two light points, while a V-shaped variant carries one. This lets you match the arrangement of light points to the profile of a street, avenue or square.
