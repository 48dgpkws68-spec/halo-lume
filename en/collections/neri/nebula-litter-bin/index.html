Nebula litter bin

The Nebula litter bin brings the design language of the Nebula system to street furniture. The bin has a round base in painted stainless steel and a fixed lid with an opening at the front. Emptying is done through a wide door, released by a lock at the front of the bag holder. This way the litter bin visually matches the lighting and the other furniture in the collection.
