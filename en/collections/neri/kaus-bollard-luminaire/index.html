Kaus Bollard

The Kaus Bollard is a lighting bollard from Neri's Landscape collection, intended for paths, green areas and other outdoor spaces. With its understated silhouette and soft, controlled light it blends into its surroundings rather than demanding attention. It is available with a 180 degree or a full 360 degree light distribution, so the beam can be matched to the location.
