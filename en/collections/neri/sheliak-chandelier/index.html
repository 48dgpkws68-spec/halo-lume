Sheliak chandelier

The Sheliak chandelier carries three luminaires from a single point, giving squares and junctions a clear lighting accent. It is built from cast iron, aluminium and hot-dip galvanised steel, with a rosette, a support tube and a central element holding three decorated arms. Each arm ends in a threaded connection onto which a surface-mounted luminaire is fitted. The result is a classic silhouette that pairs naturally with the poles and luminaires of the Sheliak family.

Three light points on one pole

The central element carries three arms, each taking its own luminaire. A single pole position thus lights a larger area, saving pole positions on squares and wide street profiles. The arms carry leaf decorations that match the rest of the range.
