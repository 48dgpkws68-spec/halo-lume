Kiosk

The Neri kiosk is a free-standing structure with a classical appearance, usable as a bar, newsstand, information point or small shop. Load-bearing cast iron columns are combined with painted steel infill panels, aluminium window frames with laminated or double glazing and external steel roller shutters. The roof rests on steel beams and is finished inside with wood panelling. Thanks to its modular design the kiosk can be adapted to function and location; the standard coating is Neri Grey or RAL 6005 green.
