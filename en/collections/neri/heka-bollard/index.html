Heka bollard

The Heka is a bollard in hot-dip galvanized steel with a base and decorative finial in cast iron. Its cylindrical shaft comes in two diameters and can be supplied with rings for attaching a chain. It lets you mark off carriageways, squares and pedestrian zones with an element that matches the rest of the Heka street furniture line.
