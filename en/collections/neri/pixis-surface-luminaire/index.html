Pixis Surface

Pixis Surface is the surface-mounted variant in Neri's Pixis range, a restrained luminaire with pure lines and balanced proportions. Its calm design allows it to blend into both contemporary and historical settings without altering their character. You can choose between the Street version for lighting routes and the Projector version for targeted accent lighting. This makes Pixis Surface a natural choice wherever mounting on a flat surface is the logical solution.
