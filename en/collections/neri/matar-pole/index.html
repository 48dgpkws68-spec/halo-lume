Matar pole

This pole by Makio Hasuike gives the Matar system its clean, contemporary base. It is built from hot-dip galvanised steel tubes with a cylindrical section and includes a service door giving access to the terminal block. A tapered and a straight version are available, both suitable for up to two light points. This makes the pole at home along through routes as well as in urban areas with a modern character.
