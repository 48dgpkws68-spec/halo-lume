Pictor bike rack

The Pictor bike rack is the bicycle parking element of the Pictor family, designed in collaboration with the architecture firm Lombardini22. Made of steel, each rack holds one bicycle. Placing several racks in a row creates parking capacity of any required size. Thanks to the shared design language, the rack sits naturally alongside the Pictor benches and bollards.
