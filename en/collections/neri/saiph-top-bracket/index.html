Saiph top bracket

The Saiph top bracket is composed of hot-dip galvanised steel elements and cast aluminium. It carries up to two light sources and forms a complete light point together with the Saiph pole and a matching luminaire. The design by Alfredo Farnè connects seamlessly with the other products in the Saiph family.
