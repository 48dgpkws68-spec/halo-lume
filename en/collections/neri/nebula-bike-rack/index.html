Nebula bike rack

This bike rack belongs to the Nebula family, the street furniture line Neri developed together with Skidmore, Owings & Merrill. It is made of steel and holds one bicycle per element. Placing several elements side by side sets the capacity you need, while the design ties in with the benches and lighting from the same line.
