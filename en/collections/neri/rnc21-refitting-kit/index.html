RNC21

The RNC21 is a refitting kit that makes existing lanterns and fixtures suitable for LED while the original luminaire remains in use. The die-cast aluminium housing can be fitted with a flat tempered glass screen, clear or prismatic, and delivers a luminous flux of up to 7,500 lumen at IP66 and IK09. What sets this kit apart is the choice between static white and tunable white, with colour temperature and luminous flux controlled together along the Kruithof curve.

Tunable white on the Kruithof curve

Besides static white, the RNC21 is available in a tunable white version. Colour temperature and luminous flux change in step with each other, following the Kruithof curve, so the light feels comfortable at every moment of the evening.
