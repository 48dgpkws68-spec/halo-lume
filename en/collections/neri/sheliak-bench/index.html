Sheliak bench

The Sheliak bench rests on cast iron side pieces that end in a lion's claw and carry floral motifs at the centre. The seat and backrest are made of brown Iroko wood. With a length of 1800 mm and a design by Domenico Neri, the bench suits historic town centres and parks that call for classic styling.
