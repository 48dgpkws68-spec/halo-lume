Castore chandelier

The Castore chandelier is a three-light crown in cast iron, aluminium and hot-dip galvanised steel for classical light columns. It consists of a rosette, a support tube and a central element carrying three brackets decorated with leaf motifs and ornamental details. Each bracket has a threaded connection for a surface-mounted luminaire, so the lighting can be specified per project. The design is by Domenico Neri.
