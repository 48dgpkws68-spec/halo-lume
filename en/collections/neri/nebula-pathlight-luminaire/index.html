Nebula Pathlight

The Nebula Pathlight is the low-level luminaire in the Nebula collection, intended for lighting paths, steps and edges in public space. The housing is made of extruded aluminium and the luminaire is available with one or two light sources. The polycarbonate diffuser reads black when the light is off, yet passes the light fully once the luminaire is switched on. This keeps the daytime image calm while the luminaire does its work after dark.

Nebula Pathlight

Black by day, light by night

The polycarbonate diffuser is black during the day and therefore barely visible in a wall or staircase. Once the light comes on, the same diffuser passes the full light output. The Pathlight thus combines a restrained daytime image with a clear evening image, with one or two light sources as required.
