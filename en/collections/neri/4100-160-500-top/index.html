Top 4100.160.500

This shepherd's crook top combines ductile cast iron with hot-dip galvanised steel. The curved arm takes the luminaire on a cast iron connector with a 3/4" GAS thread, while the supply cable runs out of sight through the arm. The result is a tidy appearance that suits classic street lighting in town centres and squares. The top accepts a single light source.
