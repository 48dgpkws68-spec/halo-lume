Nebula lighting system

Nebula is a modular lighting system for public space, designed in collaboration with the American architecture firm Skidmore, Owings & Merrill. The system comprises five luminaires: Nebula L, Nebula S, Venezia and the compact variants Compact L and Compact S. Its design adapts to contemporary as well as historical surroundings, making the system widely applicable in city centres, residential areas and parks. Around the lighting, a broader collection has been built with pathlights, two types of light bollards and matching street furniture.
