Strapitec Diamond

Our own post-top luminaire. A sculpture by day, a soft light source by night for residential streets, parks and paths.

Request a quote All specifications
A diamond on the pole

Faceted design, soft light.

The Diamond is Strapitec's own post-top luminaire: timeless, conical and faceted like a cut stone. The nano-opal cover in UV-stabilised polycarbonate gives a friendly, even light with good facial recognition for anyone walking underneath. With up to 229 lumens per watt it is also exceptionally efficient. The luminaire is built circularly, so every part can be replaced and separated.

  • Up to 229 lm/W
  • IP67 · IK10 · Class II
  • 60 mm spigot, fits almost any pole
  • Dimmable, suitable for lighting controls
  • 10-year warranty
