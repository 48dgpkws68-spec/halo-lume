Litehouse Bollard Root Mounted

The Litehouse Bollard is a robust bollard of around 1 metre high for pedestrian areas, paths and car parks. This version is root mounted directly into the ground. Choose a column in powder coated aluminium, galvanised steel with an access door, or high impact PVC, which simplifies earthing. The head and opal diffuser are UV stabilised, vandal resistant polycarbonate rated IP65 and IK10. Light is directed downwards as much as possible. Photocell, part-night dimming, Casambi and lithium emergency are optional.
