ENSO Highbay

ENSO Suspended is the high bay of the ENSO family, with a round body 670 mm in diameter and only 96 mm high. Marine grade LM6 aluminium and polycarbonate, sealed with poured gaskets, achieve IP66 and IK11. That makes it suitable for sports halls, stations, canopies, retail and industrial spaces, indoors and outdoors. At 29 to 258 W it delivers roughly 3,100 to 25,500 lumens. Maintenance is tool-free. DALI, Casambi and 3 hour lithium emergency are optional.
