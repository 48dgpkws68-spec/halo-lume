Array Surface 135

The Array Downlight Surface 135 is a compact surface downlight 190 mm in diameter and only 135 mm high. That makes it suitable for lower ceilings in offices, shops, hospitality and education buildings. Choose a lens with narrow or wide beam, an opal or microprism diffuser or a wall-washer. Bezel and reflector come in bronze, black, white or anodic brown. The die cast aluminium body is made in the UK. Outputs reach over 6,000 lumens. DALI, Casambi and wireless dimming are optional.
