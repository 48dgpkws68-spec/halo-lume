Skybay IP20

Skybay IP20 is an LED high bay for warehouses, distribution centres, shops, sports halls and airports. The range runs from the standard Skybay via the Maxi to the Maxi XL, with outputs from about 7,500 to over 65,000 lumens. Choose optics for open areas or a rack lens for narrow aisles between racking. The white steel body manages heat. It can be surface mounted or suspended from four M5 eyelets. A protective cage for sports halls, DALI, PIR and emergency are optional.
