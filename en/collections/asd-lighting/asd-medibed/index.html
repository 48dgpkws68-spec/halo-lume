MediBed

MediBed is a bedhead luminaire for hospital wards and patient rooms. It provides direct and indirect light, giving both patients and care staff good visibility without disturbing glare. The white steel body has a low glare opal diffuser and is self cooling. Lengths of 900 and 1200 mm are available, with custom widths on request. Colour rendering is above CRI 80 at 4000K. The luminaire is IP54 and achieves L90 at 50,000 hours. 1-10V dimming and Casambi are optional.
