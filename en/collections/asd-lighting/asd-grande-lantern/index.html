Grande Lantern

Grande Lantern is a generously sized wall lantern for entrances to homes, hotels and hospitality venues. The body and windows are vandal resistant, self-extinguishing polycarbonate. Because the material is UV stable and colour fast, there is no chipping or peeling paint, which keeps maintenance low. The lantern is IP44, Class I and available in black or white. The colour temperature is 4000K. A photocell is available as an option. Dimensions are 170 x 437 x 325 mm.
