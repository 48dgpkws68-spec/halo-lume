Skybeam Suspended

Skybeam Suspended is the pendant version of the Skybeam collection, designed for open ceilings, meeting rooms, classrooms and entrances with generous ceiling heights. The slim 65 mm wide profile is extruded aluminium with die cast end caps and is made in the UK. Lengths of 1180, 1480 and 1740 mm are available, with a continuous version on request. Outputs reach about 6,800 lumens. Options include 3 hour lithium emergency, eMotion Cloud wireless dimming, corridor function and Casambi.
