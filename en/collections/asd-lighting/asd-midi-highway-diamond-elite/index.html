Midi Highway Diamond Elite

Midi Highway Diamond Elite is intended for main roads, roundabouts, junctions and large car parks or business parks where more light is required. At 44 to 220 W it delivers roughly 4,300 to 27,100 lumens. The die cast aluminium body measures 672 x 329 mm, is rated IP66 and IK11 and includes auto-disconnect. The driver tray and maintenance are tool-free. It mounts post-top or side entry on Ø 60 to 76 mm, with insert reducers for smaller columns. NEMA socket, Mini Cell, PIR and Casambi are optional.
