SmartWatch

SmartWatch combines lighting and CCTV in a single solution for car parks, stations, schools, sports grounds and secure sites. The range includes five network cameras: PTZ with 30x optical zoom, Dome, Bullet with infrared up to 20 metres, Discreet with up to three cameras and Fisheye with a 360° overview. All cameras are IP66 rated for outdoor use and use Axis technology. PTZ, Dome and Bullet are designed to be visible as a deterrent, while Discreet and Fisheye stay low-key. The associated lighting delivers up to 22,000 lumens.
