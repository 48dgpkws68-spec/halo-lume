Horizon Round Plain 353mm

Horizon Round Plain 353mm is the round Horizon luminaire with an open plain ring, without louvres or shielding in front of the opal diffuser. The whole diffuser lights up, giving even all-round light with the highest output in the range: up to about 2,100 lumens at 20.7 W and up to 112 lm/W. This makes it well suited to walkways, stairwells, car parks and stations where you want generous, uniform light. Body and UV stable diffuser are vandal resistant, self-extinguishing polycarbonate rated IP65 and IK11, secured with anti-tamper screws. Three LED packages are available from 8.4 to 20.7 W. A metal base with extra side entries, lithium emergency, sensors and DALI or wireless control are available as options.
