Clarity Landscape

Clarity Landscape is a horizontal rectangular exterior luminaire measuring 276 x 190 mm for facades, entrances and outdoor areas around homes. Body and diffuser are vandal resistant, self-extinguishing polycarbonate rated IP65. A fast-fit base with gear tray and a hinged front held by a single screw keep installation and maintenance simple. The trims are interchangeable: plain, eyelid, louvre or cage. At 8.5 W it delivers up to about 520 lumens. PIR, HI-LO PIR and a photocell are optional.
