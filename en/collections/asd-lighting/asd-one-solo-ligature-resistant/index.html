One Solo Ligature Resistant

One Solo Ligature Resistant is a self-contained LED emergency downlight for mental health facilities, custodial suites and other secure areas where the risk of self-harm must be minimised. It has a ligature resistant design with a screw-fixed bezel and achieves IP65 and IK11+. The body is die cast aluminium, 125 mm in diameter, with a 68 mm cut-out. Choose an open area anti-panic optic or an asymmetric escape route optic. DALI addressable self-test is optional. Colours are white, black, bronze and anodic brown.
