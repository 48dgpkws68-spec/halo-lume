Rhapsody Anti-Vandal

Rhapsody Anti-Vandal is a 600 x 600 mm recessed luminaire for secure environments such as mental health facilities, custodial suites, healthcare buildings and car parks. The white steel body with an opal polycarbonate refractor achieves IK11 and is TP(a) rated. It suits lay-in and draw-up installation in ceilings such as exposed T-grid, but not plasterboard ceilings. At 30 to 42 W it delivers about 2,400 to 3,400 lumens at 4000K. DALI, 1-10V, plug and play, Casambi and lithium emergency are optional.
