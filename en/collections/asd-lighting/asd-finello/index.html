Finello

Finello is a contemporary linear luminaire in extruded aluminium for offices, education, healthcare, corridors and public spaces. The 200 x 55 mm profile comes in 1220 and 1520 mm lengths, with bespoke lengths on request for suitable quantities. Choose a Performance, Visual Comfort or Low Glare diffuser. It achieves IP44 and IK11+ and is made in the UK. At 14 to 59 W it delivers about 1,700 to 7,200 lumens. Surface or suspended mounting is possible. Emergency lighting, PIR and wireless dimming are optional.
