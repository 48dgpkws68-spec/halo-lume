Sign8 Zalux

Sign8 Zalux is an emergency luminaire for ATEX environments in industry, storage and process installations. Its low MESG value makes it suitable for gas groups IIA, IIB and IIC. A built-in Ni-Cd battery provides 1 or 3 hours of emergency operation. An EXIT pictogram is optional for clear escape route marking. The profile is UV resistant polycarbonate with yellow aluminium end caps and an NBR gasket. It achieves IP66 and IK10 and carries IECEx certification. The format is 485 x 157 x 173 mm.
