Invincible

Invincible is a robust cornice luminaire for high security and high dependency areas, such as custodial suites, mental health facilities, car parks and plant rooms. The steel body and 4 mm thick prismatic polycarbonate diffuser achieve IK16. It mounts in the corner between wall and ceiling and comes in 600, 1200 and 1500 mm lengths. It is IP20 as standard, with a sealed IP66 version. Outputs reach about 6,400 lumens. Options include DALI, microwave sensing, night light, Casambi and lithium emergency.
