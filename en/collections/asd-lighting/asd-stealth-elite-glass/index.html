Stealth Elite Glass

Stealth Elite Glass is the version of the Stealth Elite wallpack with a 5 mm toughened glass diffuser. It suits facades and entrances where a clean glass finish is preferred. The die cast aluminium body is rated IP65 and IK08, with a ULOR from 0.6%. Flood, wide and T-beam optics are available from 13 to 45 W and roughly 1,200 to 4,500 lumens. Photocell, PIR-controlled dimming, Casambi and lithium emergency are optional.
