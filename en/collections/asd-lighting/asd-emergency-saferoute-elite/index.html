Emergency SafeRoute Elite

Emergency SafeRoute Elite is an LED exit sign with a lightguide and opal printed 300 x 150 mm legend, with a 30 metre viewing distance. Five mounting styles are available: recessed, ceiling surface, suspended, wall surface and side entry. The steel body is white or black. A strip of 14 LEDs lights the legend evenly. Maintained or non-maintained operation is set at the wiring stage, with a lithium battery as standard. Double-sided legends, self-test and DALI are available.
