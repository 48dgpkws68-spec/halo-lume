ZIP Ligature Resistant

ZIP Ligature Resistant is a recessed downlight for mental health facilities, secure units and other spaces where ligature points must be avoided. It has a ligature resistant cover and is finished with anti-pick mastic. The pre-fitted adjustable ZIP clamp holds it in place with a few clicks, and a quick-click connector speeds up wiring. The downlight is IP65 and TP(a) rated with an opal diffuser, with a 210 mm cut-out. At 16 to 29 W it delivers about 1,400 to 2,500 lumens. DALI, corridor function and emergency are optional.
