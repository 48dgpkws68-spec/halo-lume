Storm

Storm is a weatherproof linear luminaire built to resist dust, corrosion and vandalism. Applications include multi-storey car parks, changing areas, factories, plant rooms, loading bays and construction sites. The grey polycarbonate body and opal diffuser achieve IP65 and IK11 and close with stainless steel toggle clips. Lengths of 600, 1200, 1500 and 1800 mm are available, with outputs up to about 8,700 lumens. It mounts on a ceiling or wall or from a suspension hook. DALI, corridor function, Casambi and lithium emergency are optional.
