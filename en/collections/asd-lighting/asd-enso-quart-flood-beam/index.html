ENSO Quart Flood Beam

ENSO Quart Flood Beam is the ENSO Quart facade luminaire with a wide floodlight optic behind a clear polycarbonate window. The lenses spread light broadly and evenly across facades, grounds or footpaths, so a few fittings can light a large area uniformly. The powder coated, marine grade LM6 aluminium body is rated IP66 and IK11 and has passed a salt spray test of more than 2000 hours. It is wall mounted facing down or up, with a spirit level on the back plate for a straight installation. Four LED packages from 5.8 to 31.6 W deliver around 580 to 3,420 lumens at up to 108 lm/W. A DALI driver, 10 kV surge protection and standby power below 0.5 W are standard.
