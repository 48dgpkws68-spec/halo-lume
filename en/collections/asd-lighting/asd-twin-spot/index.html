Twin Spot

Twin Spot is an LED emergency unit with two adjustable heads for larger spaces such as halls, warehouses, car parks and outdoor areas. It operates non-maintained with a 3 hour duration and delivers 610 lumens. Long life lithium batteries with deep discharge protection and automatic cut-off are used. The housing is IP65 and wall mounted, indoors or outdoors. A basic version and a version with DALI self-test are available. Photometry has been tested by the LIA.
