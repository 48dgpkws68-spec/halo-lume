Why the classic line

In a park every lantern is scenery

Visitors walk through it all day, film the main street and come back for the evening. Neri has made that scenery in Italian cast iron since 1962, for squares, boulevards and resorts around the world.

01

Genuinely cast

Cast iron around a core of hot-dip galvanised steel. It looks historic because it is genuinely cast, not themed plastic.

02

Built for crowds

Lanterns up to IK10 impact resistance, poles with an inspection hatch in the base, litter bins with a removable inner bin.

03

One design language

Pole, wall bracket, chandelier, bench, litter bin, bollard and bike rack from one family: Sheliak.

04

LED inside

The silhouette of the past with today's technology: up to 7,500 lumens, IP66, set per project for colour temperature and distribution.

Who it is for

Parks that decide for themselves

Theme parks

A main street that appears in every photo, kiosks for ice cream and souvenirs, a bandstand for shows.

Holiday parks and campsites

Paths, the central square and reception in one recognisable look, efficient LED and safe after dark.

Estates and country houses

Lanterns and pavilions that suit historic buildings, with restoration of existing poles as an option.

Zoos, Christmas markets and events

A gazebo as a photo spot, a kiosk for mulled wine, light that carries the season.
