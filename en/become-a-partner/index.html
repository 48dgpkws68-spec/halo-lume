FAQ

Good to know

Does joining cost anything?

No. There are no start-up costs, no minimum purchase and no stock for referrers and agents.

Can I keep selling other brands?

Yes. We only ask you to be open if you carry a brand that competes directly with Neri, so we can make the right arrangements together.

Who invoices the client?

For referrers and agents, Strapitec invoices. Dealers buy from Strapitec and invoice their own client.

When do I get paid?

Within 30 days after the client has paid, with a clear statement per project.

What if two partners bring the same project?

The first valid registration counts. You always receive a dated confirmation.

Can I join from Belgium, Germany or another country?

Yes, apply and choose your country. We will contact you about the options in your market.
