The Neri story
The story in five lines

How to present Neri.

01

Italian craftsmanship since 1962

Neri casts poles and lanterns in its own foundry, with in-house test laboratories.

02

Classic and contemporary

From the historic lantern for the old town to the Nebula family, designed with Skidmore, Owings & Merrill.

03

LED inside

Modern optics, insect- and dark-sky-friendly, efficient. Refit kits for existing lanterns too.

04

Restore instead of replace

Cast-iron poles last up to 50 years longer. More sustainable and often cheaper.

05

Proven in the Netherlands

Delfshaven, Utrecht, Amsterdam, Den Helder, Veenendaal and more.

Ready-to-use copy for your client

Since 1962 Neri has made lampposts, lanterns and street furniture in cast iron and aluminium in Italy, from classic lantern to contemporary family. Through us you get advice, a lighting plan with calculation and specification texts, supply and years of aftercare. See the collection at halo-lume.com/en/collections/neri/ or ask us for references near you.
