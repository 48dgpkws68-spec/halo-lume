Neri S.p.A. · since 1962

Italian craftsmanship, cast for eternity

For more than sixty years Neri has stood for lampposts, luminaires and street furniture of the highest level. Strapitec brings that expertise exclusively to the Benelux.

1962

The beginning

Neri is founded in Longiano, in Italy's Emilia-Romagna region, as a foundry for lampposts.

Foundry

In-house production

From cast iron to aluminium: drawing, mould, casting and finishing under one roof.

Lab

Tested to the extreme

In-house labs test for weathering, mechanical load and electrical safety. In the Light Room you see how light behaves before you decide.

SOM

World-class architecture

With Skidmore, Owings & Merrill, Neri developed the Nebula family: lighting and furniture from a single idea.

Benelux

Strapitec

For more than eight years Strapitec has been Neri's exclusive partner for the Netherlands, Belgium and Luxembourg.

Today

143 products

A collection from classic lantern to smart contemporary family, in cities around the world.

The foundry

Where every light begins

Neri has grown into a leading manufacturer of cast-iron lampposts and luminaires. Its own design team, foundry and test laboratories ensure that quality stays at one level, from classic lantern to contemporary family.

Light Room

See the light before it's installed

In Neri's light simulation room you experience at full scale how a luminaire behaves in public space. With Strapitec you can visit the Light Room in Italy.
