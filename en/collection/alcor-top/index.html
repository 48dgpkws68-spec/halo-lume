Alcor top

The Alcor top combines hot-dip galvanised steel, cast iron and ductile cast iron in a bracket for suspended luminaires. Up to two light sources can be fitted, so a single pole can carry two fixtures where required. Together with the Alcor pole and a classic lantern, it creates a lighting point with a traditional character, suited to town centres, avenues and squares. It has been used along the Amalfi coast, among other places.

Alcor top

Room for two light sources

The Alcor top's bracket is designed for suspended luminaires and accommodates up to two light sources. This lets you combine two fixtures on a single pole and reduce the number of poles in the streetscape. The result is a visually calmer public space.
