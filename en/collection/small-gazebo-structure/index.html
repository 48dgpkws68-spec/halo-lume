Small gazebo

The Neri small gazebo gives a square, park or viewpoint a sheltered place to linger and meet. The load-bearing structure is executed in cast iron; the roof consists of steel trusses with fibreglass roof panels and, on the inside, a wooden slatted ceiling with cast iron decorative elements. The standard finish comprises hot-dip galvanised steel with a coating in Neri Grey, RAL 6005 green or RAL 9010 white. This allows the structure to blend into historic as well as green surroundings.

Layered roof construction

The roof combines three layers: steel trusses for strength, fibreglass panels on the outside and a wooden slatted ceiling within. Cast iron decorative elements finish the ceiling, so the gazebo presents a refined image from the inside as well.
