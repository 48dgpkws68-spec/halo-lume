Top 4100.050 top

This cast iron top is fitted to the head of the pole and carries a single luminaire. It is part of Neri's modular system, in which pole, top and luminaire can be combined freely. The classic material makes it a natural fit for historic town centres and village cores; it has been used in the Italian towns of Chianciano and Locorotondo, among others.
