Heka wall bracket

This wall bracket in cold-galvanized steel allows a luminaire to be mounted on a facade where a pole is not wanted or not possible. It has a 3/4 inch GAS connection for the luminaire, internal wiring and an earthing screw. It is fixed to the wall with screw anchors and carries one suspended luminaire.
