Brenta Applique

Brenta Applique is the wall-mounted luminaire in the Brenta collection, a line of exterior lighting cast in high-performance concrete. The material, HPC, owes its strength to polymer fibres in the mix and its water resistance to a dense structure with a low water-cement ratio. Designed by EMO Design, the fixture pairs the craft character of concrete with a clean, contemporary form. Available in sizes S and L, it suits facades along squares, walkways and entrance areas.

Brenta Applique

Cast in high-performance concrete

Brenta is cast in HPC, a concrete with very high density and minimal porosity. Polymer fibres in the mix provide the mechanical strength, while a low water-cement ratio makes the material water resistant. It gives refined exterior lighting the solidity of concrete.
