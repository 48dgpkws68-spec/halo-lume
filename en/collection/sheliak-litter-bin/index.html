Sheliak litter bin

The Sheliak litter bin extends the design language of the lighting range into street furniture. The cylindrical body is made of cold-galvanised sheet steel and stands on three legs; the decorative parts and the lid are cast in aluminium. A polyethylene inner container makes emptying straightforward. Alongside the standard model there is a version with an ashtray, so you can select the right configuration for each location.
