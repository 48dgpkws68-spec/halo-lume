Nashira chandelier

Nashira is a chandelier for public space, designed by Domenico Neri, gathering four or six suspended luminaires around a single pole position. The construction combines aluminium, hot-dip galvanised steel and brass, with a rosette, a support tube and a central element to which the arms are fixed. The arms carry leaf motifs and other decorative details, and each has a threaded connection for the luminaire. This makes Nashira a natural choice for squares, boulevards and other locations that call for a representative point of light.
