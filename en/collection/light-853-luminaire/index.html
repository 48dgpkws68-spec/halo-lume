Light 853

The Light 853 is a compact lantern that combines full lighting performance with a modest size. The housing is made from die-cast, extruded and sheet aluminium, with an IP66-rated LED module and an IK09 impact rating. Its small scale makes the Light 853 well suited to streets, squares and residential areas that call for a finer point of light. Mounting is post-top or suspended.
