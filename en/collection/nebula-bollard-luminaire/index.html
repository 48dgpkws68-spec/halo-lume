Nebula Bollard

The Nebula bollard is the lighting column from the Nebula collection, designed with the American architecture firm Skidmore, Owings & Merrill. The extruded aluminium column comes in two heights, 1065 and 2740 mm, and carries one or two luminaire heads. The heads tilt from 0° to ±45° and are closed with flat tempered glass, either transparent or prismatic. This lets you aim the light precisely at paths, entrances and gathering places.

Tilting luminaire heads

Each luminaire head can be set from 0° to ±45° and is available with transparent or prismatic tempered glass. With one or two heads per column, you direct the light exactly where it is needed, without unnecessary spill towards the surroundings.
