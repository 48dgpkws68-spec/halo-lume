Nebula planter

The Nebula planter adds greenery to public space in the same design language as the rest of the Nebula collection. The planters are made of painted stainless steel and available in several sizes, including an oval version. The long version stands on three self-levelling feet, so it sits stable and level even on uneven paving. This makes the planter suitable for squares, shopping streets and other public areas where visual quality matters.
