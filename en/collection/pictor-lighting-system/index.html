Pictor lighting system

The Pictor lighting system is a lighting programme by Neri that brings together poles and lighting bollards in various sizes. The pole system comes in three configurations with heights of 4, 5 and 6 metres, and the LED units can be configured for colour temperature, luminous flux and control protocol. Cladding panels in different materials and finishes let you tune the system to its surroundings. This makes Pictor a fitting basis for municipalities and lighting designers who want one coherent lighting language throughout the public realm.

Pictor lighting system

Decorative LED module with screen printing

The system can be extended with a decorative LED module that provides ambient light and acts as a marker. Logos and graphic elements can be applied by screen printing and backlit through the module. This gives every pole its own recognisable signature in the streetscape.
