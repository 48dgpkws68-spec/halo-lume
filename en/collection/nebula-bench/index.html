Nebula bench

The Nebula bench is part of the street furniture line Neri developed with the architecture firm Skidmore, Owings & Merrill. The supports are painted steel; the seat consists of black WPC planks that are fully regenerable and carry ReMade in Italy certification. The bench comes with a backrest, with backrest and table, without a backrest, and with a partial backrest. This lets you compose the right mix of seating for every location.
