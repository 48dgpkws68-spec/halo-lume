Saiph litter bin

The Saiph litter bin has a circular build with uprights and top cover in cast aluminium and a body of deep-drawn, hot-dip galvanised sheet steel. Inside, a stainless steel ring holds the waste bag in place. The lid is hinged to the bin and locks with a key, which keeps emptying simple and prevents unwanted opening. The bin is available with or without an ashtray and visually matches the rest of the Saiph family.
