Sheliak bollard

The Sheliak bollard marks the boundary between pedestrian areas and traffic in the same design language as the rest of the Sheliak range. It is cast in ductile iron, with an octagonal base, a tapered shaft with leaf ornaments and grooves, and a sphere crowning the top. Below the sphere, rings allow a chain to be fitted if desired. Combined with poles and luminaires from the same family, the bollard helps create a coherent streetscape for historic centres.

Ready for chain barriers

Rings beneath the crowning sphere allow a chain to be attached. This lets you link several bollards into a continuous barrier without separate fittings. Without a chain, the rings simply read as part of the ornament.
