Globo

The Globo is a cast iron luminaire with an opal PMMA diffuser. Its cast iron body is decorated with ten engraved leaves, giving the fixture a classical character. The Globo comes in two versions, Globo 300 and Globo 400, and combines with poles and tops from the Neri range. This makes it well suited to historic town centres, parks and squares where visual quality and atmosphere come first.
