Nebula Venezia

The Nebula Venezia is a luminaire from the Nebula lighting system, designed by the architecture firm Skidmore, Owings & Merrill. Its transparent housing draws on the Venetian art of glassmaking, giving the fixture a clear presence in the streetscape by day as well as by night. Within the system, you combine the luminaire with a matching pole and top to form a complete light point. This makes the Nebula Venezia well suited to city centres, squares and other places where lighting helps define the character of a space.
