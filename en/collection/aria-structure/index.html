Aria

Aria is a family of gazebos with a restrained design that settles comfortably into green, urban and historic surroundings. A hot-dip galvanised steel structure carries a fixed, waterproof PVC roof, built for years of outdoor use. The collection comes in two versions: the compact Aria and the larger Aria XL, which can be combined into modular canopies for hospitality and contract projects. The result is a sheltered spot that invites people to spend time outdoors.
