Pixis Lateral

Pixis Lateral is a compact luminaire by Neri for lateral mounting, developed for streets, paths and public areas. It delivers precise light while remaining visually restrained, letting the surroundings define the scene. You can choose between the Street version for lighting routes and the Projector version for illuminating surfaces and objects. Photometric files and technical documentation support lighting calculations and engineering.
