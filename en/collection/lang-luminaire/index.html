Lang

Lang is an award-winning post-top luminaire designed by Light Bureau, part of AFRY. It combines two side-by-side light sources with four light distributions and a luminous flux from 1,500 to 15,000 lm. Through the Basic, Ready and Pro versions you choose between a single light source, five standard combinations or a fully configurable luminaire. This allows one fixture to light, for example, a carriageway and an adjacent cycle path each at its own level.

Lang

The blade: a signature of place

An aluminium screen known as the blade can be fitted between Lang's V-shaped brackets. It is laser cut per project, for instance with a municipal crest, a colour accent or a pattern that draws light and shadow onto the surroundings. Lang is available with or without the blade.
