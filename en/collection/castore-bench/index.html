Castore bench

The Castore bench is a classic park bench executed entirely in cast iron, with floral and geometric ornaments and armrests. Domenico Neri's design is modular: the bench is supplied with one, two or three elements, in lengths from 1000 to 3000 millimetres. It scales with its setting, from an intimate green pocket to a long promenade.

Modular seating

The bench is built from linkable seating elements that share one continuous design. For each location you choose one, two or three elements, setting the length from 1000 to 3000 millimetres while the classical appearance stays the same.
