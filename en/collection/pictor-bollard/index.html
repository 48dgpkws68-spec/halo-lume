Pictor bollard

The Pictor bollard is part of the Pictor street furniture range, developed in collaboration with the architecture firm Lombardini22. Made of steel, it is available in a version with a single bicycle parking space, so it guides traffic and accommodates a bike at the same time. Its understated design suits a wide variety of public spaces, from shopping streets to squares.
