Polis Suspended

Polis Suspended is the suspended version of Neri's Polis luminaire, developed for residential areas, parks, squares and city centres. Mounted in a hanging position, it retains the timeless form and careful construction that characterise the whole family. Because the light body is identical to that of the other mounting versions, the suspended configuration fits naturally into a wider Polis scheme. Certifications and photometric files are available for your lighting design.
