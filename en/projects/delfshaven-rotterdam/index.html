Refit Kit · Rotterdam

Delfshaven · Rotterdam

Same luminaires, highly efficient lighting and a longer service life: that is the philosophy behind the Refit Kit. This upgrade brings existing lanterns up to modern LED technology without costly replacement work. With the classical 'Oud Rotterdam' lantern in Delfshaven, the Municipality of Rotterdam shows that heritage and modernisation can go hand in hand.
