Privacy statement

How Strapitec handles your data.

Who we are

Strapitec, Kazemat 7, 3905 NR Veenendaal, the Netherlands, is responsible for processing personal data via this website. Questions? Email info@strapitec.com.

What data

When you use the contact form we process your name, organisation, email address, phone number and message. We only use this data to answer your question and prepare a quote if requested.

How the form works

The form is sent through the FormSubmit service, which forwards your message to our mailbox. We do not share your data with others, except with a partner manufacturer when needed for your request and with your consent.

Retention

We keep your data as long as needed to handle your request and for the legal retention period for quotes and invoices.

Cookies

This website places no tracking or advertising cookies. Your browser only temporarily remembers whether you have already seen the opening animation.

Your rights

You can view, correct or delete your data. Email info@strapitec.com. You may also lodge a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority.
