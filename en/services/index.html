Lighting advice and plans

We survey the site, think along about atmosphere and safety, and deliver a complete lighting plan with tender texts. Free and without obligation for municipalities, provinces and lighting designers.

02

Custom design with Neri

From an adapted pole base to an entirely bespoke luminaire. Together with Neri's designers and engineers, from concept to production. That is how the Flaneerkade in Den Helder got its own version of the Lang.

03

LED refitting

With RNC refit kits, existing lanterns get a modern LED interior without replacing the housing. Less energy, less maintenance and less light pollution, while the streetscape stays as it is. See Delfshaven in Rotterdam.
