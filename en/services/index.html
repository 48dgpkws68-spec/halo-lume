01

Lighting advice and plans

We survey the site, work with you on atmosphere and safety, and deliver a complete lighting plan with tender texts. Free and without obligation for municipalities, provinces and lighting designers.

02

Custom design

From an adapted pole base to an entirely bespoke luminaire. Together with our partners' designers and engineers, from concept to production. That is how the Flaneerkade in Den Helder got its own version of the Lang.

03

Restoration of historic poles

Cast-iron lampposts that last decades longer while preserving the streetscape. We survey, blast and coat, convert to LED and reinstall.

More about restoration
04

LED refitting

With refit kits, existing lanterns get a modern LED interior without replacing the housing. Less energy, less maintenance and less light pollution, while the streetscape stays as it is. See Delfshaven in Rotterdam.

How we work

Six steps to the right light

One point of contact from quote to aftercare. Direct lines to the manufacturer.

01

Introduction & survey

Together we visit the site and discuss its history and your ambitions for atmosphere, safety and sustainability.

02

Lighting plan & calculation

A lighting plan with calculations, product selection and colour temperature per zone. Including tender specifications.

03

Trial set-up

Experience the light in your own public space before you decide. Or visit Neri's Light Room in Italy.

04

Production

Our partners produce in their own factories in Italy and the United Kingdom, with in-house test laboratories. Custom work is possible.

05

Delivery & handover

We coordinate delivery and installation with your contractor, right up to the first night the lights come on.

06

Aftercare

Spare parts, extensions and refitting for decades to come. One point of contact.
