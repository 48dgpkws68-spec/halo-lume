About Strapitec

Light for public space

Strapitec advises on, supplies and maintains lighting for municipalities, provinces, architects and contractors. From Veenendaal, across the Netherlands.

We believe good light makes a city safer, more beautiful and more human. That is why we only work with products we would choose ourselves.

Who we are

Expertise, from plan to pole.

For more than eight years we have worked on public space in the Netherlands. From the canals of Utrecht to the boulevard of Den Helder. We work with you on atmosphere, safety and sustainability and make sure the light looks right for decades.

Our clients are municipalities, provinces, lighting designers, landscape architects, developers and infrastructure contractors.
