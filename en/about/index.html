About us

Italian craftsmanship, Benelux expertise

Strapitec is Neri's exclusive partner for the Benelux. We advise, supply and guide from first sketch to aftercare.

We believe good light makes a city safer, more beautiful and more human. That is why we work with the best manufacturer we know.

The partnership

Eight years of exclusive Neri

For more than eight years we have been Neri's exclusive partner for the Netherlands, Belgium and Luxembourg. Local advice, short lines to Italy and one point of contact from quote to aftercare.

Our clients are municipalities, provinces, lighting designers, landscape architects, developers and infrastructure contractors.
